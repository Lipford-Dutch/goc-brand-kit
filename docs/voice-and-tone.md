# Voice and tone

**Tagline:** Order the chaos.

**Supporting line:** A proving ground for infrastructure, observability, commerce, civic systems, and the ideas we refuse to leave on paper.

**Close:** Build. Observe. Iterate.

## Who we are on the page

Generals of Chaos is a technology, web and digital strategy studio in Carolina Beach, NC. The voice is field-ops, not think-tank: theaters, deployment status, field reports, command center. Short sentences. Concrete verbs. Proof sits next to the claim.

## Do

- Lead with the work, then the method.
- Prefer "ships", "runs", "observes", "documents" over "leverages" or "synergizes".
- Mark maturity honestly: alpha, demo, private beta, planned, shipped.
- Keep client and private lanes unnamed on public surfaces until they are cleared.
- Use sentence case in body copy. Headlines may be uppercase, following the HQ site.

## Don't

- Don't invent product names, dates, certifications or legal suffixes.
- Don't describe a demo as a government or certified production service.
- Don't write like a generic SaaS landing page.
- Don't use the WTFManuals palette (deep blue / bright red) or any other project's kit in place of this one.
