# Logo and color usage

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of the amber corner squares (one-eighth of the mark).
- Minimum mark size: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print.
- The wordmark and horizontal lockup may scale down until the "GENERALS OF CHAOS" eyebrow is still readable (about 10 px).

## Do

- Use the full-color mark on `#07090a` or photography dark enough that the white "G" still contrasts.
- Use the inverse lockup on `#f4f5f3` or light photography.
- Use the monochrome mark when only one ink is available.
- Keep the amber corners on the full-color and inverse marks. They are part of the letter, not decoration you can recolor freely.

## Don't

- Don't stretch, outline, add shadows, or rotate the mark.
- Don't recolor the amber corners to the green, or swap the field and the letter, except by using the provided inverse or mono files.
- Don't place the dark mark on a dark photograph without a `#07090a` panel behind it.
- Don't set the wordmark in a font other than Impact / the documented fallbacks.
- Don't lock the mark up with another company's logo without a written co-branding decision.

## Color contrast

- `#f4f5f3` on `#07090a` is the default reading pair.
- `#e65f00` is for accents, rules, and status — not for long body text on the dark field.
- `#3ee6a2` is for operational status (live, passing, healthy), not for headlines.
